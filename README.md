<p align="center"><strong>Empowering Heroes Through Community & Resilience</strong></p>

Not ALONE is an innovative platform created to bridge the gap between lone soldiers and a supportive community. Our mission is to ensure that every soldier has access to essential resources, heartfelt guidance, and a network of caring volunteers. Whether it's donations, sharing meals during Shabbat, or simply connecting with someone who understands, our user-friendly app makes it easy for lone soldiers to receive the help they need—because no one should ever have to face their journey alone.
